About this project

Project objective

AQI Typing Adventure teaches players about their state's 2023–2025 air quality through a five-scene story narrated by Skye, a friendly pigeon. Typing text is generated once per state via Claude Haiku 4.5, cached in S3, and reused for every future player of that state — so each state gets a consistent, grounded, kid-friendly telling of its own air quality data.

How to use

  1. Pick a state on the Play tab or hit Surprise me.
  2. Read Scene 1 (Skye's intro), then type your way through Scenes 2–4.
  3. Your WPM, accuracy, and time appear in the top-right as you type. Skye's location and your state's 2025 air-quality KPIs sit in the panel on the left.
  4. Hit Submit score now any time to skip ahead to the score card — unfinished scenes are excluded from your average.
  5. On the score card, save your score with a username to appear on the Scores board.
  6. Visit Map to see which states Skye has visited.

Score calculation

Each typing scene is scored on a 0–100 scale.

  • Per-scene score = 60% × accuracy + 40% × min(100, WPM).
  • Total score = mean of the scenes you attempted. Skip a scene or submit early? It's excluded from the average — no penalty.
  • Stars: <55 = ★, 55–64 = ★★, 65–74 = ★★★, 75–84 = ★★★★, ≥85 = ★★★★★.

Accuracy is weighted more than speed on purpose — Skye would rather you land clean than crash fast.

Data source

US Environmental Protection Agency. Air Quality System Data Mart [internet database] available via https://www.epa.gov/outdoor-air-quality-data. Accessed May 09, 2026. Pollutants: PM2.5 (param 88101), Ozone 8-hr max (param 44201). 2025 data may be preliminary.
